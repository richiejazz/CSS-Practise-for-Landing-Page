About

Designed around how light makes you feel.

Most lamps are either on or off. Aura treats light as atmosphere: a calm backdrop for working, winding down, or having friends over.

  1. Calm

    Colours blend slowly, so changes feel natural rather than distracting.

  2. Considered

    A minimal form that sits quietly on a desk, shelf or bedside table.

  3. Personal

    Save your favourite scenes and return to them with a single turn.

Do I need the app to use Aura?

No. The dial on the lamp switches scenes and adjusts brightness. The app adds custom colours and timers.

Can I create my own scenes?

Yes. Pick any colour in the app, set the brightness, and save it to the dial alongside the built-in scenes.

Is the light comfortable for evenings?

Scenes like Dusk and Midnight are designed to be dim and warm, and the wind-down timer fades the light out gradually.

How do I care for the shade?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid solvents or abrasive cleaners, which can mark the frosted finish.
